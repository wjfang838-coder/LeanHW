1. Module and ports



3. Reset

The module has no reset input and no state elements. Both outputs are combinationally defined from the current inputs at power-up and at all later times. Before any reset edge could have been seen, shut_off_computer already equals cpu_overheated, and keep_driving is 1'b1 exactly when arrived is 1'b0 and gas_tank_empty is 1'b0; neither output is unknown or retained. There is no first step after reset release because reset does not exist.

4. Timing

shut_off_computer is a continuously evaluated combinational output equal to cpu_overheated, with zero clock-cycle latency. It has no pulse, hold, clear, completion, or restart phase.

keep_driving is a continuously evaluated combinational output of arrived and gas_tank_empty, with zero clock-cycle latency. It has no pulse, hold, clear, completion, or restart phase.

The circuit has no clock, periodic output, physical period, or delay law. For the evaluated full-cycle trace, row k is observation step k after combinational settling; it does not denote an implicit 10 ns clock tick.

5. Behaviour

At all times, cpu_overheated directly determines shut_off_computer: cpu_overheated equal to 1'b0 produces shut_off_computer equal to 1'b0, and cpu_overheated equal to 1'b1 produces shut_off_computer equal to 1'b1, independently of arrived and gas_tank_empty, including simultaneous changes and repeated or interrupted input patterns, with no mode, transaction, or retained effect.

At all times, arrived equal to 1'b0 permits keep_driving to equal the logical complement of gas_tank_empty, while arrived equal to 1'b1 forces keep_driving to 1'b0, independently of cpu_overheated, including simultaneous changes and repeated or interrupted input patterns, with no transaction or retained effect.

When arrived is 1'b0, gas_tank_empty equal to 1'b0 produces keep_driving equal to 1'b1 and gas_tank_empty equal to 1'b1 produces keep_driving equal to 1'b0. When arrived is 1'b1, gas_tank_empty has no observable effect because keep_driving remains 1'b0, including simultaneous changes and repeated or interrupted input patterns with no retained effect.

Every input is a one-bit scalar, so the preceding cases cover every possible input encoding. The two outputs update independently and continuously; there are no disabled modes, transactions, cancellations, stored results, starts, or terminal states.
